# Diabetes Diagnostic Criteria

Use the following diagnostic criteria as the primary evidence for classification.

- Diabetes is diagnosed if HbA1c is 6.5% or higher.
- Diabetes is diagnosed if a current/random plasma glucose value is 200 mg/dL or higher.
- Do not apply the fasting plasma glucose threshold unless the measurement is explicitly described as fasting.
- Values below these thresholds may indicate no diabetes or a prediabetic range, but the final answer should still be a binary YES or NO.

For this experimental condition, prioritize these diagnostic criteria over less relevant contextual details.
